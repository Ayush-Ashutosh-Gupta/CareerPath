# **App Name**: CareerPath

## Core Features:

- Adaptive Career Quiz: An intelligent, multi-question system that guides users through career interests, where subsequent questions and options dynamically adjust based on previous responses for highly relevant career recommendations.
- Comprehensive Career Explorer: A searchable and filterable directory providing access to detailed profiles for over 80 diverse career paths.
- Detailed Career Profiles: In-depth views for each career, showcasing daily activities, essential skills, competitive exam details, salary ranges, and an 8-step roadmap to success.
- Personalized Results & Roadmaps: Displays quiz results with top career matches, personalized roadmaps, and initial options for re-evaluation, based on quiz scores.
- AI-Enhanced Career Comparison Tool: A side-by-side career comparison feature, integrating an AI tool to generate nuanced insights, such as unique pros and cons, when contrasting two selected careers.
- AI-Guided Mistake Recovery: A system to assist users who feel they've chosen an incorrect academic stream, utilizing an AI tool to provide clear rationales and customized alternative career pathways.
- Responsive & Intuitive Interface: A clean, modern, and highly responsive user interface designed for seamless experience and optimal readability across all device types (mobile, tablet, desktop).

## Style Guidelines:

- The primary color is a rich, modern purple (#6126D9). This hue evokes ambition, intelligence, and clarity, ideal for guiding career choices.
- A light, muted background color (#F2F0F4) provides a clean canvas, maintaining a subtle connection to the primary purple without distraction.
- The accent color is a dynamic blue (#4763EB), offering strong contrast for interactive elements and highlights, suggesting trust and forward momentum.
- Headlines use 'Space Grotesk' (sans-serif), lending a modern, tech-informed, and slightly bold aesthetic to key information. Body text utilizes 'Inter' (sans-serif), chosen for its excellent legibility and neutral, objective character, ideal for detailed career content.
- Modern, minimalist line icons are used to convey clarity and functionality. Emojis are integrated thoughtfully to add personality and visual cues for career profiles and quiz results, as specified in the prompt.
- The design prioritizes responsive adaptability, transitioning from a single-column layout on mobile, to a two-column structure on tablets, and an optimized multi-column grid on desktop. Comparisons are side-by-side on larger screens and stacked for mobile ease-of-use.
- Subtle and fluid animations are employed for transitions between pages, loading states, and interactive elements, ensuring a polished and engaging user experience without distracting from content.
